# Procurement procedure (source document)

**`SoMSQP PRO 01 2026.pdf`**: "Quality Procedure — Procurement and Inventory Control", SOMSQP-PRO-01, Issue 02, Rev 00, 06-07-2026, School of Maritime Studies, Centurion University. Prepared by the Head of Compliance; approved by the Director.

The PDF is in this folder (added 17-11-2026).

How it maps to the app, and the owner's decisions on it, are in `../06-new-modules.md` § B. Read the PDF itself for:
- the wording of each form (FL1–FL10) and its fields;
- retention periods (§7) and the file structure and naming (§7.1);
- KPIs (§8) and ISO 9001 requirements (§9).

Summary of what the PDF says, for when the file isn't at hand:

| § | Point |
|---|---|
| 4 | Roles: Procurement Manager, Procurement Executive, HODs, Accounts Team, Director / Principal, Building Security Supervisor. Also named: Store In-charge (5.9, 5.11), Dean (5.6), Quality Manager / Head of Compliance (5.6.3, 9.2), Head of Accounts (5.7) |
| 5.4 | At least two quotations from registered vendors |
| 5.5 | HOD approves → Procurement Manager reviews and forwards → Accounts approves per DoFA |
| 5.6 | Urgent: critical item or a Dean/Director request; direct Dean or Director approval; one quotation allowed; FL10; monthly review; recurring item → CASE log |
| 5.7 | PO copy to the Principal and Head of Accounts; the vendor acknowledges |
| 7 | Retention: FL1 5 y · FL2 relationship + 3 y · FL3 3 y · FL4 5 y · FL5 8 y · FL6 5 y · FL7 3 y · FL8 3 y · FL9 asset life + 5 y |
| 7.1 | File name: `[Document Ref]-[Category]-[Year]-[Version]`, e.g. `SOMSQP-PRO-01-FL5-PO-2026-v1` |
| 8 | KPIs: registered vendors and changes (annual); urgent requisitions (monthly); urgent closure (target 48 h); procurement findings / NCs (annual) |
| 9 | Quarterly department feedback (below 80 % → corrective review); NCs in the CASE log within 7 working days; staff training; continual improvement |
